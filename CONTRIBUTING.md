# Contributing to Weave

Contributors will propose changes to this repository after accepting the contributor agreement below, and each change is held to the engineering standard that follows. Weave is not accepting outside contributions yet: the agreement and its acceptance mechanism need legal review before public contribution intake opens.

## Contribution rights

Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions. Before RTEPL accepts a contribution, the contributor must accept the separate [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). That agreement gives RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited permanent project-use, distribution, sublicensing, and relicensing rights, including the right to offer the combined project under commercial terms without later renegotiation or approval.

A Developer Certificate of Origin is not a substitute for this agreement. Accepted contributions follow the outbound licence of their owning source home:

- original engine, application, adapter, interface, integration, first-party domain, Rust SDK and tool work uses `AGPL-3.0-or-later`;
- emitted contracts, generated types and genuinely thin language bindings will use `Apache-2.0`;
- inherited or third-party material retains its applicable licence.

The Apache interoperability boundary must not absorb compiler, permission, graph, runtime, reconciliation or effect logic. Such behaviour belongs in the AGPL implementation.

## Engineering standard

A contribution must preserve the repository's existing ownership boundaries and describe only what exists. Build and check it as [the README](README.md#build-and-check) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) describe. Preserve third-party provenance and applicable notices.
